Computer Science Student Support

Students working on final-year projects are encouraged to discuss their proposed scope and technical approach with their project supervisor.

Useful early questions include:
What problem am I solving?
Who experiences the problem?
What evidence shows the problem exists?
What is the smallest useful version I can build?
How will I evaluate whether the solution works?

For software projects, students should consider source control, testing, documentation, security and reproducibility from the beginning rather than leaving these activities until the end.
